I am a Ph.D. candidate in Traffic Information Engineering and Control at Chang'an University in Xi'an, expecting to graduate in January 2027. Before that, I completed an M.S. in Computer Science at Bahria University, Islamabad, and a B.S. in Computer Science at the University of the Punjab, Lahore, which gave me a strong foundation in computer science. My research focuses on four themes: autonomous vehicles, trajectory prediction, path planning, and unmanned systems, and my results have been published as journal articles, conference papers, and book chapters with Springer Nature, Elsevier, IEEE, and CRC Press. I have also taught computer science at schools in Islamabad for more than three years, supported by a Bachelor of Education. Outside academia, I like to sketch and paint in oils.

Research themes

Learning-based methods that help vehicles, vessels and drones perceive, predict and plan.

01

Autonomous vehicles

Interaction-aware models that help autonomous vehicles understand surrounding traffic and plan safe behavior on shared roads.

02

Trajectory prediction

Graph convolutional and transformer models that forecast how vehicles and UAVs will move through space and time.

03

Path planning

Reinforcement learning, including spatial-aware self-attention DQN, for planning paths in static and dynamic environments.

04

Unmanned systems

Learning-based planning for unmanned surface vessels (USVs) and UAV base stations, including 3D trajectory planning.

Other works

EV charging demand forecasting, electricity load forecasting, time-series forecasting and computer vision.
